Create a complete mobile-first UI/UX design system and app prototype in Figma for an interactive magical ritual app called “Virtual Altar”.

The app is NOT horror-themed, dark occult, realistic witchcraft, or gothic fantasy.

The emotional direction is:

* cozy
* comforting
* dreamy
* playful
* magical
* emotionally soothing
* soft feminine energy
* relaxing bedtime app energy
* journaling app mixed with magical girl aesthetics

The experience should feel like:
“a peaceful digital altar space where users perform symbolic self-care rituals and manifestation activities through interactive guided spell experiences.”

The visual world should feel cohesive and emotionally immersive across ALL screens.

---

## OVERALL ART DIRECTION

The visual design should combine:

* pastel magical girl aesthetics
* cozy witchcore
* soft anime-inspired UI
* dreamy journaling apps
* calming mobile game interfaces
* celestial bedroom aesthetics
* soft stationery design
* tiny floating charms
* cute collectible object design

The app should feel:

* warm
* inviting
* emotionally safe
* slightly whimsical
* elegant but playful

DO NOT use:

* realistic occult imagery
* dark gothic fantasy
* horror elements
* heavy textures
* sharp UI
* aggressive glow effects
* cluttered fantasy decorations
* realistic flames or dark ritual circles
* scary symbols

Instead use:

* simplified symbolic illustrations
* cute animated magical objects
* soft vector art
* floating decorative particles
* rounded playful interfaces
* dreamy gradients
* translucent layered cards
* soft glow bloom effects
* clean spacing

6. INTERACTIVE RITUAL MODE

---

This is the MAIN FEATURE.

Design a fully immersive guided drag-and-drop ritual experience.

IMPORTANT:
The experience should feel like:

* an interactive magical tutorial
* relaxing symbolic self-care
* cozy ritual simulation

NOT:

* difficult gameplay
* puzzle solving
* chaotic sandbox

Interaction structure:

* only ONE active interaction at a time
* glowing highlighted target areas
* smooth guided progression

Example flow:

STEP 1:
Instruction:
“Place the rose candle in the center.”

User drags candle.

When placed:

* candle softly glows
* petals float outward
* tiny sparkle burst appears
* next object unlocks

STEP 2:
“Scatter the petals around the flame.”

Petals animate beautifully after dragging.

STEP 3:
“Write your intention.”

User types:
“I trust myself fully.”

The app generates:

* glowing sigil
* magical symbol animation
* soft pulse effect

STEP 4:
“Seal the ritual.”

Ribbon wraps automatically around altar card.

Completion animation:

* bloom effect
* floating stars
* magical ambient particles

The ritual interface should contain:

* draggable bottom toolbar
* altar table center stage
* ambient animated background
* calming instruction panel

The draggable toolbar should feel:

* toy-like
* cute
* tactile
* satisfying
